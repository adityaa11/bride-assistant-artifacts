## Outcome and scope

Deliver one versioned policy contract and environment-neutral retention/lifecycle behavior that development, testing and later production integration consume. Separate policy authority/evidence from environment configuration, configuration validation, and validated runtime values. Domain logic consumes validated policy values, classified proof facts, clock and lifecycle ports; it does not branch on environment names or ingest approval documents. Production requirements are authoritative. Development/test may use shortened intervals, controlled clocks, synthetic data and disposable adapters only through validated profiles.

T07 owns retention/classification decision behavior, applicable start events, active-hold non-deletion, scanner outcome/fail-closed contracts, profile and adapter validation, activation denial, and disposable byte/metadata/audit/history verification. It records each unresolved D-03 decision and approval slot without inventing a value. It does not implement production upload endpoints, administrative UI, scanner infrastructure, Neon byte/SQL adapters, provider backup/restore, or release controls. The accepted T06 `DocumentStore.put/read` byte contract remains intact; an injected lifecycle deletion port sits above it, with M4-W01 owning its production implementation.

## Invariants and configuration contract

1. Eight calendar months from final proof review, or upload if never reviewed, applies only to eligible operational proof. Applicable Indonesian statutory records have at least ten years under the approved classification/start rule. Unknown applicability or missing approval denies production deletion/activation; T07 does not decide which real proof/audit records qualify. Controlled-clock production-duration cases run under isolated test identity with authoritative duration values; they never activate the production profile.
2. An active accountable hold prevents deletion in every profile. Scanner clean/infected/unavailable/timeout outcomes retain the same fail-closed/quarantine semantics in every profile; no provider is chosen here. Proof-specific metadata/audit expires with eligible bytes; booking status history remains independent.
3. A trusted deployment boundary establishes environment identity. Production rejects short development durations, controlled clocks, test/disposable adapters and missing approvals. Development/test reject production database/object endpoints, credentials, real customer data and any unknown or incompatible environment/adapter combination. Validation occurs before side effects; no untrusted environment variable alone confers authority.
4. Policy authority is versioned requirements, decision approvals and evidence. Environment profiles contain runtime values and adapter selection. Validation binds both against policy constraints. Shared domain behavior receives only the validated result and ports, never legal documents or an environment-name conditional.
5. Private bytes and audit records stay out of public URLs and artifacts. Five-minute rehearsal is not production retention. No unapproved hold exception, scanner, RPO/RTO or legal classification is assumed.
