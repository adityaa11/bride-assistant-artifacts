## Outcome and exact scope

Publish a **versioned, evidence-backed fixture relationship audit** covering T01–T18, not merely booking. Enumerate the shared facts that public `/`, customer, and admin modules are meant to consume as separate projections: catalogue items/bundles/packages, promotions, portfolio/testimonials/events and publication, customer identity and ownership, bookings and snapshots/status, admin customer directory, availability, and T18 media metadata/bytes. Include fixture-only review states and explicit non-authoritative command results. Classify T01–T03 infrastructure/adapter/UI ownership, T05–T07 superseded history, and T17 review; do not silently omit tickets with no domain records.

For each domain, trace the current producer or literal, route/scenario ID and adapter version, record IDs and relevant fields, every consuming module, relationship to the same underlying business fact, and the accepted M1 versus M2+ boundary. Distinguish (a) genuinely shared source/projection, (b) independently copied or contradictory fixture data, (c) intentional state variant or privacy/publication projection, (d) deferred backend-only relationship, and (e) prohibited storage/authority or unresolved planning question. Audit the **way data was created**, including direct fixture imports, component-local arrays, route-local projections, generated records, browser storage, fixture media associations, and scenario catalogue snapshots; one central file alone does not prove a shared fact.

The report's required output is a finite, prioritized **list of fixtures needing refactor**, each with stable finding ID, exact producer and consumers, conflicting IDs/fields or missing link, source and route evidence, accepted authority, proposed bounded refactor objective, affected ticket(s), proof needed, and successor. Also list relationships that already work and those intentionally deferred, so the inventory does not imply that every screen must share every field. Do not implement or approve a refactor in this ticket.
